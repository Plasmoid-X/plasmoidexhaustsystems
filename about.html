About

13 years on the water, before this was ever a business.

Plasmoid X was founded by a yacht engineer who spent years maintaining marine engines and wanted a cleaner, longer-lasting way to run them.

The Founder

Built by a yacht engineer

Ricardo Negri founded Plasmoid X after more than 13 years as an engineer on sailing and motor yachts, looking after their engines day to day.

Plasmoid X oversees every installation, and any contractors we bring in are vetted to the same standard. Support continues after the install is finished.

Core Values

What we build the business on

Reliability & trust

We guarantee our work. If something's wrong, we put it right at no extra cost or inconvenience to the vessel, and we stay available to clients after installation.

Quality & longevity

We work with machinery built to last and can spot a shortcut a mile off. We use good parts, install them precisely and check the workmanship ourselves.

Sustainability

The business exists to reduce air and water pollution. Parts and materials are built to last a vessel's lifetime with minimal maintenance, so they don't need replacing.

Transparency

One price covers the full installation, with no surprises and no upsells.
