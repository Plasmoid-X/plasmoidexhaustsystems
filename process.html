How We Work

From first conversation to finished install.

Every retrofit follows the same six steps. We design around a scan of your engine room, and nothing happens to your vessel until you sign off.

The Process

Six steps, start to finish

1

Initial Meeting

We meet to agree a plan of action: what the vessel needs, what you expect and realistic timeframes.

2

Site Visit & 3D Scan

A Plasmoid X representative visits the vessel to inspect the machinery and 3D scan the engine room. The design is built around that scan.

3

Design & Drawings

We produce the designs and drawings in house from the scan.

4

Approval

We send the designs and drawings to the vessel for review. Work only goes ahead once you approve them.

5

Scheduling

We plan where and when the installation happens, during the vessel's downtime.

6

Installation & Handover

Plasmoid X oversees every installation, and we stay available after handover.
