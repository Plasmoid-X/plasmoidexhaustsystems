Exhaust after‑treatment wasn't built for yachts

SCR and diesel particulate filter systems are bulky, high-maintenance and prone to failure. They add weight, take up engine room space and give the crew another system to service at sea.

Plasmoid X works on the air before it reaches the engine.

  1. 1

    Air in

    Fresh air is drawn into the system instead of going straight into the turbo.

  2. 2

    Ionizer

    The air bubbles through water under UV light, which turns it into plasmoids. Filters keep the water out of the engine.

  3. 3

    Vajra

    The plasmoid air spirals through two linked stainless spheres around a sealed core.

  4. 4

    Heat exchange

    Hot exhaust spirals the other way through the outer chamber and warms the plasmoids through the wall.

  5. 5

    Into the engine

    The warmed plasmoids feed the turbo, where they are designed to give a more complete burn.

  6. 6

    Exhaust out

    The exhaust leaves through the outer chamber, cooler, and out of the boat.

What it is designed to do: burn less fuel for the same work, send cleaner exhaust out of the boat, and cut the soot that wears cylinders and internals.
