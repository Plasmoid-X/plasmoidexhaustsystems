Ready-Made Reports

Department ReportsOpen a department in Departments, then Report: any month or period, as a PDF or Excel, with the assistant's summary.

Master's Report (PDF)

The whole vessel for the month: a summary, the vessel programme, crew changes, a section per department (work done, what was outstanding at the end of the month with the reason given, defects, purchases and spend, stock, hours, logbook, SOPs, ISM) and Safety & ISM. Read by the Captain, Support and the DPA only. Prepare: give the reasons, then make the PDF. The assistant can make it too. On the 1st the Captain, Support and the DPA are asked for the reasons; the report is made automatically on the 7th if nobody has made it, and filed under Documents.
