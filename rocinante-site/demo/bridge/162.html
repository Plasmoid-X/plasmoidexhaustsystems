ISM Checklists

For the Captain, senior officers, the DPA and Support.

Where Safety & ISM Tasks Go

Corrective-action tasks, new joiners' familiarisation and the yearly internal audit are filed in this domain (a corrective action can pick another).

Internal Audit Checklist

One question per line: the ISM Code element, a bar, then the question ("7 | Is bunkering done by the checklist?"). A new audit copies this list; each audit can add its own questions too.

New Joiner's Familiarisation

One item per line. A new joiner's first trip (from the rotation) raises a task with this checklist on their join day, for their head of department.
