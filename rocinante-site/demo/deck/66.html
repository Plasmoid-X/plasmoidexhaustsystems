These are planning figures; your employment contract decides pay. A handover day is worked by both — each earns a day of leave. Travel days are paid, no leave. Call-outs (called back between rotations) are worked days on top of the rotation, not part of the balance; their flights don't count against the allowance. Flights are one-way: one out to the boat, one home. Payout = HO + travel + call-out days, plus difference + carried when that's a surplus; a shortfall isn't taken off the payout. Once a year is marked paid out it is settled: days worked over the partner were paid, a shortfall is written off, nothing carries into the next year, and that year's trips are locked. Marking paid out uses the approved trips only. Total days covered: 121.

Plan Ahead

Drafts the next trips from the last approved one (no trip shorter than about a month): shared dates taken in turns (see Shared Dates), equal days per year (carry-over included), significant days at home, flights within the allowance, no changeover during a no-changeover charter or owner trip, and people home for their important dates where possible. Earlier drafts are replaced. Nothing counts until you approve it.

Takes a few seconds.

Significant Days

Up to three dates each that come round every year (a child's birthday, an anniversary). The planner tries to have you home on them, where it can. Only the person and the rotation's planners see the reasons.

Shared Dates

Dates this rotation's partners share, like Christmas or New Year. Ticked: you take turns being home for it each year. Unticked: the planner just avoids a changeover during it. Leave it empty for no turn-taking at all.
